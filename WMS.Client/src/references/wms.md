## Project Overview

Warehouse operations depend on accurate inventory records, efficient stock movement, and reliable order fulfillment. As operations grow, managing these processes through disconnected tools and manual workflows becomes increasingly difficult.

We developed a comprehensive, cloud-hosted **Warehouse Management System (WMS)** designed to bring essential warehouse operations together into one centralized platform.

The solution provides businesses with tools to manage products, warehouse locations, inventory movements, receiving, order dispatch, reporting, and user access through a modern web application.

Built with scalability, security, and operational efficiency in mind, the system demonstrates how custom software can simplify complex warehouse workflows.

## The Challenge

### Warehouse Operations Can Become Complex Without the Right Systems

Many warehouse businesses face operational challenges when inventory, orders, and stock movements are managed manually or across multiple disconnected systems.

Common challenges include:

- **Limited Inventory Visibility:** Difficulty maintaining accurate records of stock quantities across warehouses and storage locations.
- **Manual Processes:** Time-consuming receiving, adjustments, and stock transfer workflows.
- **Inventory Errors:** Duplicate transactions, inaccurate stock updates, and insufficient-stock issues.
- **Disconnected Order Fulfillment:** Difficulty coordinating inventory availability with customer orders and dispatch operations.
- **Limited Accountability:** Inadequate tracking of stock movements, user activities, and operational changes.
- **Inefficient Reporting:** Time spent manually collecting inventory and transaction data for analysis.

These challenges informed the design of a unified platform focused on visibility, control, and reliable warehouse operations.

## Our Solution

### One Centralized Platform for Warehouse Operations

We designed and developed a full-stack Warehouse Management System that connects inventory management, warehouse operations, and order fulfillment within a single application.

The platform provides a structured workflow from product registration and stock receiving through inventory transfers, order preparation, and dispatch.

Instead of relying on disconnected records, warehouse teams can manage daily activities through a centralized interface with controlled access and detailed transaction histories.

Our solution emphasizes three core principles:

**Operational Efficiency:** Streamline repetitive warehouse processes through structured digital workflows.

**Data Accuracy:** Protect inventory integrity through transactional updates, stock validation, and idempotency controls.

**Visibility & Accountability:** Provide inventory reporting, movement history, audit logs, and role-based access.

## Key Features & Capabilities

### 01. Product & Warehouse Management

Manage products, categories, warehouses, and storage locations from a centralized interface.

Organize inventory across multiple warehouse locations while maintaining structured product and location records.

**[Image: Products and warehouse locations screens]**

### 02. Real-Time Inventory Tracking

View current inventory balances and stock distribution across warehouses and storage locations.

Track inventory transactions to understand how stock quantities change through receiving, adjustments, transfers, and dispatch.

**[Image: Inventory tracking screen]**

### 03. Inventory Receiving & Adjustments

Record incoming stock and manage inventory increases or decreases through controlled workflows.

Stock validation, atomic database operations, and idempotency protection help prevent inconsistent or duplicate inventory updates.

**[Image: Goods receiving or stock adjustment interface]**

### 04. Warehouse Transfers

Move inventory between storage locations, including locations belonging to different warehouses.

Each transfer generates corresponding stock movement records, providing traceability for both the source and destination.

**[Image: Warehouse transfer interface]**

### 05. Order Management & Dispatch

Manage customer orders through a structured lifecycle, from draft creation to preparation and dispatch.

The system validates stock availability and updates inventory during dispatch using transactional operations, helping maintain consistent inventory records.

**[Image: Orders management and dispatch screens]**

### 06. Barcode Generation & Scanning

Generate Code 128 barcodes for products and storage locations.

Support barcode-based identification and lookup to simplify warehouse item and location identification.

**[Image: Barcode labels and scanning interface]**

### 07. Reports & Data Export

Access reports covering stock levels, inventory movements, receiving, adjustments, transfers, and dispatched orders.

Apply filters and export operational data as CSV files for further analysis and recordkeeping.

**[Image: Reports interface]**

### 08. Secure Access & Audit History

Control access through four user roles: Administrator, Warehouse Manager, Warehouse Operator, and Viewer.

The application includes invitation-based onboarding, authenticated API access, role-based permissions, and administrator-only audit history with automatic retention management.

## Our Development Approach

### Designed for Reliability, Security & Maintainability

**1. Modular System Architecture**

We structured the backend using Clean Architecture principles, separating core business rules from database access and external integrations.

**2. Reliable Inventory Transactions**

Inventory operations use database transactions, concurrency-aware updates, stock availability validation, and idempotency protection to help maintain consistent stock records.

**3. Secure User Access**

We integrated token-based authentication, invitation-controlled access, and API-enforced role permissions to restrict operations according to user responsibilities.

**4. Modern User Experience**

We developed a responsive React interface with structured navigation, operational dashboards, data tables, and dedicated workflows for warehouse activities.

**5. Cloud Deployment**

We containerized the frontend and backend independently using Docker and deployed them to Microsoft Azure, with managed PostgreSQL database hosting.
